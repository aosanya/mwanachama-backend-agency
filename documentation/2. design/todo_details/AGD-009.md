# AGD-009 — Objectives attach to workflows; RACI is a label on a step

Superseded in part by [accountable-role.md](../accountable-role.md) (ISS-179, owner decisions D39 and D49): the label, the `role_assignment` row and its operations are gone and a step names one `accountable_role_id`. The Workflow to Objective part below still stands.

Owner decision of 2026-10-31. Supersedes the Objective-scoped RACI of
[AGD-001](AGD-001.md) (decision 1, AG12) and the work item's required
`objective_id`.

## Why

Role↔Objective was a link nobody kept: the roles that serve an objective are
already the roles on the steps of the workflows that deliver it. The relation people reason in is
Workflow↔Objective, and RACI is something a person picks on a step.

## The shape

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Workflow↔Objective | implicit, through each step's `objective_id` | `workflow_objective { workflow_id, objective_id }`, many-to-many, both in the same draft |
| `work_item.objective_id` | required | removed; a step takes its objectives from its workflow |
| `role_assignment` | `{ role_id, objective_id, label }` | `{ work_item_id, role_id, label }`, the step in the same draft |
| Role↔Objective | stored | gone; read through steps and workflows |

`label` is `responsible`, `accountable`, `consulted` or `informed`. On a step
the builder picks any number of roles and gives each a label: a human role
that is consulted has Consulted selected.

## Rules

1. **No limit on any label, Accountable included.** The "at most one
   accountable" refusal and its publish check are deleted. The only refusal
   left is a duplicate: the same role with the same label on the same step.
   One role may hold two labels on a step (Responsible and Accountable).
2. **The label is only a label.** It does not drive behaviour. Who acts, who a
   form or notice goes to, who answers a yes/no and the time limit stay in the
   step's own fields (`role_id`, `recipient_role_id`, `kind`,
   `time_limit_minutes`) as in [workflow-steps.md](../workflow-steps.md). The
   approval gate reads those fields, not the RACI rows.
3. **The scan does not police RACI.** How many steps carry an Accountable, and
   who, is the workflow builder's call. The checks "Objective with no
   Accountable" and "two Accountables" are deleted, which closes the
   simultaneous-create race AG53 tracked.
4. **"No untagged work" moves to the workflow.** A workflow with no objective
   is a blocking `error`, where a work item with none was before.
5. **Dangling references are checked** for both new joins: a
   `workflow_objective` whose workflow or objective is gone, and a
   `role_assignment` whose step or role is gone. Deleting a workflow, an
   objective, a step or a role removes its join rows in the same transaction,
   as AG27 did for the cascades it missed.
6. **Fork and publish carry both joins.** Fork remaps both ids of each join;
   the published document gets `workflow_objectives`, and its
   `role_assignments` entries name a step.

## Import of an older document

`work_items[].objective_id` seeds one `workflow_objective` per distinct
(workflow, objective) pair. An old objective-level `role_assignments` entry
has no step to land on and is ignored, not an error; a template picks its
labels up again when it is next mined.

## Screens

The Roles list loses its "Objectives" column and gets no replacement; a column
there, if one is ever wanted, counts workflows, not steps. Each row shows the
role's description on a second line under its name (owner, 2026-10-31). A role opens a details page, with an
Edit button that opens the role's form. A workflow shows its objectives and an
objective shows its workflows; a step shows its roles with their labels.

### Data model

- `workflow_objective { id, draft_id, workflow_id, objective_id, created_at,
  last_updated, deleted }`, declared in `agency.blueprint.json` and carried in
  Go by `models/workflowobjective.go`. Many-to-many, both ends in the same
  draft.
- `role_assignment { id, draft_id, work_item_id, role_id, label, ... }`
  (`models/roleassignment.go`). It names a step and a role and has no
  objective. The four labels are unchanged.
- `work_item` has no `objective_id`; a step takes its objectives from its
  workflow. `CreateWorkItem` and `UpdateWorkItem` refuse a `workflow_id` that
  is empty, missing or in another draft (`ErrWorkflowNotFound`).
- Errors: `ErrDuplicateRoleAssignment` (replaces `ErrDuplicateAccountable`),
  `ErrWorkflowObjectiveNotFound`, `ErrDuplicateWorkflowObjective`. Filters:
  `RoleAssignmentFilter { Label, WorkItemID, RoleID }` and
  `WorkflowObjectiveFilter { WorkflowID, ObjectiveID }`.

### Manager methods

`AgencyManager` gains `CreateWorkflowObjective`, `GetWorkflowObjective`,
`ListWorkflowObjectives` and `DeleteWorkflowObjective`
(`workflowobjective_impl.go`), and `ListRoleAssignmentsForWorkItem`, which
replaces `ListRoleAssignmentsForObjective`. The role assignment methods keep
their names and now take a step (`roleassignment_impl.go`); create validates
the step and the role and inserts in one transaction, and update classifies a
duplicate. Deleting a workflow removes its `workflow_objective` rows and the
`role_assignment` rows of its steps; deleting an objective removes its
`workflow_objective` rows; deleting a step or a role removes the
`role_assignment` rows naming it, all in the same transaction.

### Operations and tools

| Operation | Route | Tool |
|---|---|---|
| `create_workflow_objective` | POST `/workflow-objectives` | `agency_link_workflow_objective` |
| `list_workflow_objectives` | GET `/workflow-objectives` | `agency_list_workflow_objectives` |
| `get_workflow_objective` | GET `/workflow-objectives/{workflow_objective_id}` | `agency_get_workflow_objective` |
| `delete_workflow_objective` | DELETE `/workflow-objectives/{workflow_objective_id}` | `agency_delete_workflow_objective` |
| `list_work_item_raci` | GET `/work-items/{work_item_id}/role-assignments` | `agency_list_role_assignments_for_work_item` |

Actions are `agency.workflow_objective.create`, `.list`, `.read`, `.delete`
and `agency.work_item.list_raci`. `list_objective_raci` and
`agency_list_role_assignments_for_objective` are gone. `agency_set_raci`
(`create_raci`) and the other four `raci` operations keep their names; the
create body is now `{ work_item_id, role_id, label, draft_id }`.

### Two unique indexes

- `role_label_unique` on `role_assignment (work_item_id, role_id, label)`,
  restricted to rows that name a step. Two writers racing cannot both add the
  same label for one role on one step; the loser reads as
  `ErrDuplicateRoleAssignment`. Rows kept from before AGD-009 name no step and
  are left out, so the index can be built over a table that still holds them.
- `wf_obj_unique` on `workflow_objective (workflow_id, objective_id)`. The loser
  of a race reads as `ErrDuplicateWorkflowObjective`.

### Document, publication, fork, scan, brief

- `document.go`: the published document carries `workflow_objectives`
  (`id`, `workflow_id`, `objective_id`) and `role_assignments` entries that name
  a step. The document hash covers both.
- Publication and the draft fork (`publication_impl.go`, `draft_impl.go`) copy
  both joins and remap both ids of each; codes stay stable across forks.
- `issue_impl.go`: a workflow with no objective is a blocking error; a
  `workflow_objective` whose workflow or objective is gone, and a
  `role_assignment` whose step or role is gone or whose label is not one of the
  four, are blocking errors. The Accountable checks are deleted.
- `role_brief_impl.go`: the brief states each objective through the workflows
  the seat serves, keeps only the seat's own steps, and shows the labels the
  seat holds on them. Issue and objective comments follow the same wording.

### Older databases

`Provision` (`provision.go`) runs on every start.

- When the table set or the `wf_obj_unique` index is missing, it first collapses
  exact duplicate `workflow_objective` pairs to the oldest row, then migrates,
  so the unique index can be created.
- For every step that still holds the older `objective_id` column, it seeds one
  `workflow_objective` per distinct draft, workflow and objective, when both
  rows exist in that draft and no link exists yet. A second run adds nothing.
- Old objective-level `role_assignment` rows, and the old `objective_id` data,
  are kept untouched. They name no step, so the scan reports each as dangling
  until the owner decides what becomes of them (pending item 8 below).

### Import of an older document

`work_items[].objective_id` seeds one `workflow_objective` per distinct
(workflow, objective) pair, alongside any `workflow_objectives` the document
carries. A `role_assignments` entry with an objective and no step is skipped,
as is one whose step or role is unknown or whose label is invalid. None of
these is an error. Unknown keys never error. The fixture for the company's own
agency in `testdata` is the AGD-009 shape.

### Pending owner confirmation

Decisions taken by agents during the build and not yet confirmed by the owner.

1. **Approval gate precedence (api-shared).** The gate that decides who
   approves a step uses the step's acting `role_id` first and then
   `recipient_role_id`, not an Accountable label, in line with rule 2. Nobody
   confirmed this order.
2. **Objective-level RACI in the mined templates was dropped.** Across the 634
   mined templates, all 14,192 objective-level RACI entries were removed.
   About 7,435 of them were single-step ones that could be restored with
   `migrate_templates.py --keep-single-step`.
3. **The template builder in wakala-api refuses a workflow with no
   objective**, matching the blocking scan error.
4. **Old-shape template files that remain** import with their objective links
   but without RACI: 33 under `mwanachama-fundraising/search/*/proposed-agency`,
   the 3 hub demo templates, the hackathon `proposed-agency`, and the console
   `public/templates`.
5. **Stale names kept.** `accountable_role_id` and `AccountableRoleID` in
   api-shared still carry the old name and were left alone.
6. **Postgres row locks.** `CreateRoleAssignment` and `CreateWorkflowObjective`
   validate inside a transaction but take no row locks (`FOR UPDATE`) at
   read-committed. The two unique indexes close the duplicate race; the
   check that the step, role, workflow or objective still exists is not
   protected against a concurrent delete.
7. **PATCH can still set `deleted: true`** (AG57) on both new objects, as on
   every other object.
8. **Existing databases keep their old objective-level `role_assignment`
   rows**, flagged as dangling by the scan until the owner decides to delete
   them or to restore them onto steps.
