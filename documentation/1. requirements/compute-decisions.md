# Decisions on calculating steps

Numbered, never renumbered. Each decision is stated as a rule, without the example it was asked with. The engine-side decisions (the formula language, rounding, currency places, library versions) are in `mwanachama-backend-compute/documentation/1. requirements/decisions.md`. Open questions stay on the page they belong to, with the page's proposed answer.

| # | Decision | Consequence | Settles | Issue | Who |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The builder picks a source for each input of a calculating step from a list: an answer from an earlier step, a field of a record, a ledger balance, or a fixed value | Inputs are never matched by name on their own, because a question is known by its wording and rewording one would silently disconnect an input | Inputs | ISS-19 | Owner 2026-11-17 |
| 2 | The builder chooses, per calculating step, who runs it: the agency automatically, or a named role that confirms the worked-out figure first | A confirming step needs a role that exists in the draft | Who runs it | ISS-19 | Owner 2026-11-17 |
| 3 | A confirming role's outcomes are routes the builder draws: accept, send back, or a route to an overwrite step | The step has no built-in overwrite; overwrite exists only where the builder wires it | Confirmer's power | ISS-19 | Owner 2026-11-17 |
| 4 | In a bulk run the confirming role confirms the whole run once, as a list with totals and the unusual figures first, and may hold back individual rows; nothing is posted until it is confirmed | The list is part of the calculating step page | Bulk confirmation | ISS-19 | Owner 2026-11-17 |
| 5 | Every calculating step must name what happens when it cannot work out a result, whether the inputs were bad or the calculation failed. The two choices are: go to a step the builder names, or stop and tell the person what is wrong | A step with no choice is a blocking error in the Issue scan. In a bulk run each failed row follows the step's choice. A failed calculation is a business outcome and spends no failure budget | Failure | ISS-6 | Owner 2026-11-17 |
| 6 | Balances come from a ledger: double-entry, append-only entries, a correction is a reversing entry, and a balance is the sum of entries. A calculating step works out an amount; a separate posting step posts it | Nothing stores a balance that can drift | Ledger | ISS-7 | Owner 2026-11-17 |
| 7 | A figure that is not money is kept in a separate append-only record of figures beside the ledger: one entry per subject, figure and date, carrying how it was worked out | The ledger stays money only; the history of a figure can be read and each entry traced | Non-money figures | ISS-7 | Owner 2026-11-17 |
| 8 | When a stopped bulk run is started again, the person is asked each time: keep the rows already done and run the rest, or cancel the finished rows and run everyone again | Cancelling a finished row leaves a reversing entry in the ledger | Stopped run | ISS-17 | Owner 2026-11-17 |
| 9 | A workflow can hold a calculated value before it becomes real: a calculating step may hold its result until an approval or posting step releases it | A held figure is not a ledger entry and not a record of figures; where it lives, expiry and who may release are open on the held figures page | Held figures | ISS-22 | Owner 2026-11-17 |
| 10 | When a held figure is opened for approval after its inputs have changed, it is worked out again from the current numbers, both figures and the reason are shown, and the approver approves the new figure or sends it back | A held figure is never posted unchanged when its inputs have moved | Rework at approval | ISS-22 | Owner 2026-11-17 |
